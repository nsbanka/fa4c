The students facing financial problems in continuing their education from 6th to 10th standard will be supported by our organisation. We mostly prefer orphans and semi-orphans. We support students only from Government Schools (Municipal Corporation Schools / Zilla and Mandal Parishad Schools) / Government Aided Schools. All the education related programmes conducted by us are considered as a part of 'Vidyabhyasam'.

## Our Activities

- Financial help towards the education (6th to 10th Standard) of the students.
- We mostly support orphans and semi-orphans studying in Government schools.
- Distribution of school supplies, uniforms, and educational materials.
- Regular follow-up with students and their families to ensure continued progress.

## Eligibility

- Students from 6th to 10th standard
- Studying in Government / Government-aided schools
- Preference given to orphans and semi-orphans
- Family facing genuine financial hardship
