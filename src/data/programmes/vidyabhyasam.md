The students facing financial problems in continuing their education are supported by our organisation. We supported the individual education of 400+ students so far. We even donated education supplies and materials in bulk to the students studying in government schools. All the education related programmes conducted by us are considered as a part of 'Vidyabhyasam'.

## Our Activities

* Financial help towards the education of the students. We prioritize orphans and semi-orphans students.
* Distribution of school supplies, uniforms, and educational materials.
* Providing library and computer lab facilities to the students of government schools.
* Voluntary activities and support to the visually challenged students.
* Regular follow-up with students and their families to ensure continued progress.
