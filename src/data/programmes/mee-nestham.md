We support the promotion of rich Indian culture and even encourage the skills of talented individuals. We promote cultural activities, sports, arts, and skills through this programme.

Apart from the four programmes (Vidyabhyasam, Pragathi, Sphoorthi, and Sankalpam), all other activities done by us are considered as a part of 'Mee Nestham' — meaning "Your Friendship" in Telugu.

## Our Activities

1. Cultural events celebrating Indian heritage and traditions.
2. Sports competitions and physical fitness camps.
3. Supporting talented artists, musicians, and performers.
4. Community celebrations and public awareness events.
5. Skill showcases for youth with special abilities.
