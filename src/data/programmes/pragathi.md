We work for environmental development. All the programmes conducted by us related to environmental development are considered as a part of 'Pragathi'.

## Our Activities

1. Plantation programmes at housing areas, schools, and colleges.
2. Environmental awareness drives and workshops.
3. Partnering with local communities to maintain green spaces.
4. Encouraging eco-friendly practices among youth and students.
5. We even support and encourage organic farming practices.
