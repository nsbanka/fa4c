A person who helps the other person during a medical emergency always stands as an inspiration to the rest of the people. All the medical related programmes we do are considered as a part of 'Sphoorthi'.

## Our Activities

1. Blood donation drives and camps.
2. Organ donation awareness programmes.
3. Medical health camps for underprivileged communities.
4. First aid and health awareness workshops.
5. Partnering with hospitals and medical organisations for outreach.
