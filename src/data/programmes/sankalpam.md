The women who are in distress will be supported by our organisation. We will show a way to lead their life. Such women who expect our support to lead their life should have strong determination towards the way of employment shown by us. Thus this programme is named as 'Sankalpam'. All the women empowerment programmes conducted by us are considered as a part of 'Sankalpam'.

## Our Activities

1. Vocational training and skill development workshops.
2. Connecting women with employment opportunities.
3. Financial guidance and microfinance awareness.
4. Self-defence and confidence-building programmes.
